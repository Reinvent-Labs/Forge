## What is Forge?

Forge is a multi-agent AI platform built for early-stage startups that don't have a full engineering team. It runs 24/7 in the background, autonomously handling the technical work so founders can focus on growth.

## What Forge Does

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="eye" title="Monitors production 24/7">
    The **Watcher agent** continuously polls your error tracker, uptime monitor, and metrics. When something goes wrong — a spike in error rates, slow response times, a failed deployment — it detects it within minutes and triggers the right response agent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="wrench" title="Fixes bugs autonomously">
    The **Fixer agent** performs root-cause analysis, identifies the failing code, generates a patch, opens a PR, and notifies you for approval. Simple fixes ship without you touching the keyboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="hammer" title="Ships features on your roadmap">
    The **Builder agent** takes feature requests from your backlog and implements them — writing code, tests, and documentation. You review and approve before anything merges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="shield" title="Keeps your codebase secure">
    The **Security agent** runs secret detection, SAST scans, and dependency audits on every push. Critical vulnerabilities are flagged immediately with plain-English explanations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="chart-line" title="Tracks technical debt">
    The **Debt agent** scores your codebase weekly, identifies the highest-risk areas, and queues up remediation tasks — prioritized by business impact.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The Approval Model

Forge never acts unilaterally on high-impact changes. Every significant action — merging a PR, running a migration, changing infrastructure — goes through an **approval step**.

You approve or reject in under 30 seconds via:
- The **Forge dashboard**
- A **Slack message** with one-click buttons
- A **Telegram command**

Low-risk actions (opening a PR, posting a comment, running a scan) execute automatically in `FULL` autonomy mode.
